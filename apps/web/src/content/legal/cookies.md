## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember things, such as the fact that you are signed in, between page loads and visits.

## 3. Analytics and marketing cookies

Our cookie banner has two optional categories:

- **Analytics** — to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
- **Marketing** — to measure advertising campaigns and show relevant ads.

**No analytics or marketing tools are installed at the moment, and no such cookies are set.** The categories exist so that your choice is recorded in advance.

If we decide to add such tools in the future:

- we will first list each cookie in this policy, with its provider, purpose and duration, and update the effective date;
- we will set them **only if you have given consent** for that category;
- if you have already made a choice, we will ask you again where the change requires it.

## 4. Your choices

- **Cookie banner.** When you first visit, you can accept or reject optional categories. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
- **Changing your mind.** You can change your choice at any time through the "Cookie settings" link in the website footer **[TO CONFIRM: link exists]**. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything that happened before.
- **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics and marketing cookies and as an opt-out of any sale or sharing of personal data.
- **Browser settings.** You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop parts of the site from working.
