## 2. Current status

The Service is **partially conformant** with WCAG 2.1 AA: some parts do not yet fully meet the standard. **[TO CONFIRM: status after the first accessibility audit.]**

### What we do

- Semantic HTML and headings, so pages work with screen readers.
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
- Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images hidden from assistive technology.
- Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA.
- Animations respect the "reduce motion" setting of your system.
- Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.
