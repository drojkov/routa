## 1. Model providers

When you send a request, its content (prompts, files and parameters) is transmitted to the provider of the model you choose, which generates the response. We do not send your name or email address to model providers. Each provider handles the data under its own terms and may retain it for a limited time, for example for abuse monitoring.
